Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

CloudCompass Books (the "App") is a private, read-only reporting tool operated by CloudCompassAI ("we", "us"), at quickbooks.cloudcompassai.com. It displays the transactions, Profit and Loss statement and Balance Sheet from the operator's own QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to the general public. This policy explains what the App does with data.

Who uses the App

The App has a single authorized user, the business owner, who signs in with a username and password. There are no public sign-ups.

Information we access

  • QuickBooks Online data. After you approve access at Intuit, the App reads accounting reports from your QuickBooks company through the Intuit QuickBooks Online Accounting API: transaction lists, Profit and Loss, and Balance Sheet. These reports may include payee names, memos, account names and amounts.
  • Access credentials. To keep the connection working, the App stores the Intuit OAuth access token, refresh token and company (realm) ID. They are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on our server, outside public web access.
  • Sign-in and security data. A session cookie keeps you signed in. We keep a short-lived record of failed sign-in attempts, stored as a one-way hash of the IP address, to block password guessing.

What we do not do

  • We do not copy QuickBooks report data into our own database. Reports are fetched from QuickBooks when you open a page and are shown to you, not stored.
  • The App is read-only. It never creates, edits or deletes anything in QuickBooks.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics. The App has no advertising or third-party analytics scripts.
  • We do not access, store or see your bank login credentials. Bank connections are made inside QuickBooks Online.

How information is used

Only to display your own accounting reports to you and to keep the QuickBooks connection and sign-in secure.

Service providers

The App is hosted on HostGator shared hosting in the United States. QuickBooks data is provided by Intuit Inc. and is governed by Intuit's own Privacy Statement. We do not share your data with any other third party.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. QuickBooks tokens are encrypted at rest. Configuration and token files are blocked from web access. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what is collected so there is little to expose.

Retention and disconnecting

Stored tokens are kept only while the connection is active. You can disconnect at any time using Disconnect on the QuickBooks connection page, which deletes the stored tokens, or by revoking the App's access from your Intuit account (QuickBooks Online → Apps / Connected apps). Failed sign-in records expire after 15 minutes.

Children
